# Case study: three scalars on one fixed tumour neighbourhood

Data: BC-TCGA, 529 breast tumours. Fixed molecular space: the 1000 genes with the highest variance across tumours, each standardised; Euclidean distance; kNN graph. Normals (61) are used only for the healthy reference of the deviation score. Generated by `python3 -m comparison.scalar_case_study`.

Reference setting (fixed before looking at results): k = 15, f smoothed x2 on the graph, peak persistence threshold 0.04 of the range of f; basins with at least 20 tumours are reported. Stability grid: k in [10, 15, 20, 30] x smoothing in [0, 1, 2] x threshold in [0.02, 0.04, 0.08] (36 settings). A basin counts as found in a setting when some basin there shares at least half of the samples (Jaccard >= 0.5).

Deviation check: healthy model of 2 components; deviation median 130 for tumours, 66 for held-out normals (1 of 61 held-out normals above the tumour median).
